Memory
Also called Pairs or Concentration, this is the classic children's game where players flip over two face-down cards at a time, trying to find matching pairs from a grid set out on a table; if they match, they keep the pair and go again, but if not, the cards are flipped back, and play passes to the next person, with the goal being to collect the most pairs by the end, developing focus and recall. Skills Developed: Focus, visual memory, concentration, and recall. Options: Vary the card size using a slider Specify the number of pairs to play with, from 2 to 26 Choose the shape of the grid; e.g. for 6 pairs: 1x12, 2x6, 3x4, 4x3, 6x2, 12x1 Choose to have the shape determined to be as wide as the screen will allow Play Solitaire or against the Computer: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Expert Timing: Untimed Timed (Lose Turn or Random Turn) with the user choosing the max time for a move Statistics: Moves/Matches/Accuracy/Score/Time Recorded best results for each user: Solitaire  : Moves/Accuracy/Time vs Computer: Score/Accuracy Incidentally, when I was about 12 years old and my brother was 10, we once played a game of Memory with 42 packs of playing cards, placing them all over the house; that's 1,092 cards.
BlockWords
A 9-letter word, which has a suitable number of subwords that can be made with 3, 4 or 5 letters, is randomly selected for the user to find the subwords. 10 subwords of each length, not all of them. The use of the middle letter is not required. The images of the 9 letters are displayed in a 3x3 rectangle, and the successful subwords are added to a list in alphabetic order; the counts of successful words are displayed above their respective lists. The numbers of words available in the list of allowable words is also displayed at the top of each list. No letters are shown in this game. Check the words in the lists to avoid attempting a word already found. There is no timing in this version. When there are more than 80 words, you may continue to Mastering this game will help you become a master of reading fingerspelled words. Buttons: New/Cancel/Check/Back/Hint
Reader
This app provides for reading fingerspelled words in a sentence. It is akin to both software for training for speed reading and software for increasing typing speed. Provide some text, press the Start button, and start reading and typing what you read or opt to just read what you see. A slider allows you to increase or decrease the speed. You may repeat the exercise as often as you like. Options: Type/Not Type Speed Size of images Display horizontally or as frames
